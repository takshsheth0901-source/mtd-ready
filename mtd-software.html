Choosing Making Tax Digital software

You need software that HMRC recognises for MTD for Income Tax. HMRC keeps the official list on GOV.UK: search “Find software that’s compatible with Making Tax Digital for Income Tax”. Here’s how to narrow it down.

The three types

TypeBest forWatch out for
Bookkeeping appMost people. Connects to your bank, captures receipts, sends updates.Monthly fees vary a lot; check what’s in the cheapest plan.
Bridging softwarePeople who already keep good spreadsheets.Your spreadsheet must still be kept digitally and linked, not retyped.
Free toolsSimple businesses with few transactions.Fewer features. Some are only free with a particular bank account.

Questions to ask before you choose

  • Does it handle your income: self-employment, property, or both?
  • Is it on HMRC’s list for Income Tax, not just VAT?
  • Can it send your end-of-year tax return as well as quarterly updates?
  • Does it connect to your bank, so transactions come in automatically?
  • Can you snap receipts on your phone?
  • What does it cost per month, and does the price go up after an introductory offer?
  • Can your accountant log in to it, if you use one?

Check your bank first

Some business bank accounts include bookkeeping software at no extra cost. Ask your bank before paying for a separate app.

Don’t leave it to April

Set the software up a few weeks early and record a month or two of income and costs. You’ll find any problems while there’s still time to fix them.
