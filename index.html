Do you need Making Tax Digital, and when?

From 6 April 2027, sole traders and landlords with over £30,000 of income must keep digital records and send HMRC an update every quarter. Check in 30 seconds whether that includes you.

How do you earn?
Yearly income from self-employment and property

Add both together. Use takings or rent received before expenses. Don’t include wages from a job, pensions or savings interest.

Common questions

Is the £30,000 before or after expenses?

Before. It’s your total income from self-employment and property added together, before you take off any costs or allowances.

Do I file more tax returns?

No. You send four short quarterly updates of income and expenses through your software, then your tax return at the end of the year as before. The updates aren’t tax returns and you don’t pay tax with them.

Which year’s income decides when I start?

For April 2027 it’s your income in the 2025–26 tax year (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026). For April 2028 it’s 2026–27.

Is MTD Ready part of HMRC?

No. We’re an independent service. We don’t give tax advice; the accountants we introduce you to do. Always check your own position on GOV.UK.
