Privacy notice

This explains what we collect when you use MTD Ready, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

runs MTD Ready and is responsible for your data. Contact: .

What we collect

  • What you type in the help form: name, email, phone (if given), postcode, what help you want, and any message.
  • Your checker answers: whether you’re a sole trader or landlord, and your income band.
  • The date and time you sent the form.
  • If you join a waitlist: your name and email.

The checker and the bank statement tool run entirely in your browser. Your bank statement is never uploaded or stored, and nothing you enter in the checker is kept unless you send a form.

Why we use it

To introduce you to one accountant who can help with Making Tax Digital, and to follow up about that introduction. If you join a waitlist, we use your email only to tell you when that product launches. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form.

Who we share it with

  • One partner accountant, chosen for your area and needs. They may pay us a fee if you become their client. This doesn’t change what you pay.
  • Our service providers, who store the data for us: Supabase (database) and GitHub Pages (website hosting).

We never sell your details or add you to marketing lists.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after your enquiry, so we can track the introduction, then we delete it. Ask us and we’ll delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent at any time, by emailing . If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
